Unofficial ToneCommand MK1 experimental AI builder

This is NOT the previous read-only test and NOT a production MK1 release.
Use a NEW folder. Do not overwrite your old TC checkout or change its guard.

1. Install official Windows Python3.12 64-bit if not already available.
2. Open Install-MK1-AI-Trial.cmd; wait for offline checks to pass.
3. Close old TC normally, close Axe-Edit, then Start-MK1-AI-Trial.cmd.
4. In AI SETTINGS, choose your own signed-in CLI or API/backend, then SAVE.
5. Select your own genuinely empty preset and scene 1 manually on the MK1.
6. CONNECT MK1, enter that test preset number, and type your request.
7. GENERATE PLAN -> REVIEW CHANGES -> CONTINUE TO CONFIRM.
8. Keep external monitoring muted. Confirm all three conditions, then SEND.
9. Stop on ANY error; do not repeat a partial/uncertain write or edit a guard.
10. On verified completion DISCONNECT MIDI, then inspect with Axe-Edit.
11. After independent confirmation, lower physical listening level first,
    unmute and audition gently. Do not Store in this first trial.
12. DOWNLOAD TEST REPORT and share your reviewed summary. Do not post keys.

Try: Create one scene inspired by Pink Floyd's Wish You Were Here album,
for an expressive spacious lead. Choose an amp/cab and suitable chorus,
delay and reverb. No NAM, fuzz, pitch, controllers or permanent storage.

Only this recorded MK1 FW33 Beta2 fingerprint is supported. A mismatch is
a stop, not a request to update firmware or pretend your unit is MK2.
See docs/MK1-AI-TRIAL.md. This trial proves neither an exact record match
nor safe physical loudness; the first MK1 writes remain experimental.

What does External 1 or 2 mean?

On your Fractal you choose which physical pedal or MIDI-CC feeds an External controller. TC binds that source to the parameter but does not change the global setting. No fixed CC number is assumed.

Wah: Off disables Auto Engage; Fast Pos is the verified Auto Engage option. Volume uses Auto Engage Off. Check operation with your own pedal.

Pitch: an existing Pitch 1 on channel A becomes Advanced Whammy, heel at the original note and toe one octave higher. Choose External 1 or 2 explicitly. Auto Engage stays Off; SEND activates Pitch in the current scene. Other scenes using A share its sound. Custom curves are not overwritten.

Delay, amp gain, other sources, custom modifier curves and removing existing bindings require separate verification. No binding wanted? Leave an unbound block unchanged. For now remove an existing binding on the device or in Axe-Edit after closing this connection.
